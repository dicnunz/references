# Attribution and distribution rights

Original helper code, instructions, and authored comparison artifacts are MIT licensed. Third-party works retain their stated rights. Inspection notes are original observations scoped to the recorded location and medium; they do not transfer ownership of a source.

The catalog preserves work URLs, collection attribution, and per-record rights. A link-only record authorizes no redistribution. No restricted article, game, or interactive source is bundled in full.
