B06: Fold a specimen-label tent

Create a printable cut-and-fold template and concise assembly instructions for a small museum specimen-label tent, using SVG in a self-contained HTML page. It must be geometrically buildable and visually coherent at real scale. Required net: four contiguous panels, each 80 mm wide, with heights 40 mm, 40 mm, 40 mm, and a 10 mm glue tab. Three 40 mm panels form a triangular-prism tent by joining the tab inside the first panel. Net total is 80 by 130 mm. Distinguish outer cut lines, interior fold lines, and glue area with a legend. Put readable specimen copy on one visible 80 by 40 mm panel: “River-worn slate”; “Collected beside the Alder footbridge”; “Study collection · 2026”. Include a 50 mm calibration bar. Fit on one A4 page at 100% print scale. Observable acceptance: dimensions and fold boundaries are inspectable in SVG, print preserves physical dimensions, no required copy lies on the glue tab or crosses folds, instructions identify the joining edges, and a small assembled-view diagram agrees with the net.
