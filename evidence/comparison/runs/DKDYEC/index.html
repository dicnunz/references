Forty-eight years of making

1948–1996

A coat fastened. A shirt finished. A uniform ready. Behind each button were hands that learned the weight, checked the edge, and counted the next batch. This exhibition brings the objects back alongside the people who made them, giving room to the skill, attention, and everyday lives held in small things.
