A boat rises.
Water falls.

This lock lifts a boat 3 metres.
The secret? Let water from the upper canal flow downhill into the chamber. No pump lifts the boat.

Gates: doors for boatsPaddles: small water openingsSIDE VIEW · SIMPLIFIED · FOLLOW 1 → 3
1

Enter. Then enclose.

At 0 m, the boat enters through the open lower gate. Close that gate and the lower paddles. The upper gate stays shut.

2

Let the water in.

Open the upper paddles. Water flows downhill from the upper canal. With both gates closed, the chamber fills. The floating boat rises with the water.

3

Match. Then leave.

Wait until the chamber reaches 3 m, matching the upper canal. Close the upper paddles, open the upper gate, and float out.

Why wait before
opening a gate?

Unequal water levels push against the gate. Opening the upper gate early would unleash a rush of water into the chamber. Only open gates when the water levels on both sides match.
