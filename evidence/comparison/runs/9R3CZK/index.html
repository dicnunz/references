Field notes / an imagined place

A minute in the marsh

A little time to notice what is already here.

One minute · four small observations

Let your attention come to rest.

Begin whenever you are ready. There is no sound.

01 · Near reeds
02 · Water rings
03 · A bird
04 · The whole
01

Near reeds

Follow one reed from water to tip. Beside it, another leans a little further. Imagine the smallest bend passing through their stems.

02

Water rings

Something unseen has touched the water. One ring opens into another. Follow the outer edge until it is difficult to find.

03

A distant bird

A small bird crosses above the far bank. Let your eye travel with it, then stay with the space it leaves behind.

04

The whole marsh

Let the reeds, the water, and the open sky arrive together. Nothing needs to hold your attention now. Stay here as long as you like.
