Field notes / The canal lock

A boat goes up.
Water goes down.

This lock lifts a boat 3 metres.
The trick? Let water from the upper canal flow into the chamber.

Gate: boat doorwayPaddle: water valve→Water flow
1

Enter & enclose

Enter through the open lower gate. Close it behind the boat, and close the lower paddles.

2

Let water in

Open the upper paddles. Water flows downhill from the upper canal into the chamber. The floating boat rises with the water.

3

Wait for a match

Wait until the chamber reaches 3 m, level with the upper canal. Water stops flowing in when the levels match.

4

Open & leave

Now open the upper gate. The boat floats out into the upper canal.

The lifting force

No pump lifts the boat. Gravity brings the water in; the rising water supports and lifts the boat.

Why wait to open a gate?

Unequal water levels push harder on one side of the gate. Opening early would let water rush through. Only open gates when the levels on both sides match.
