A ThousandSmallClosures

Objects and stories from the Vale Button Works

Saturday 17 October, 2–5 pm

Old Packing Hall, 8 Canal Lane

A button is small enough to overlook. Making it well takes an eye for a clean edge, an even hole, a sound finish. From 1948 to 1996, the Vale Button Works made buttons for coats, shirts, and uniforms. This exhibition brings the objects back beside the people who knew them by touch, by sight, and by the thousand.

For former workers, their families,
and everyone who would like to listen.

Bring a button, a photograph, or a story. Admission free.
